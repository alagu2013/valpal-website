VALPAL — STANDALONE WEBSITE

Extract the complete ZIP, then double-click index.html. Keep the Assets folder next to index.html. No internet connection, web server, package installation, or build step is needed.

Assets/style.css — visual design
Assets/js/script.js — slider, navigation, search, counters, and inquiry interactions
Assets/images/ — supplied logo, favicon, and three generated editorial images
Assets/fonts/ — locally bundled Manrope font files

Search, sliders, FAQs, and counters run offline. The contact form validates input and downloads an inquiry text file; it does not send information. Login and signup buttons show a clear client-access notice and do not collect passwords. Connect a real account service and inquiry delivery before enabling these online.

Content is proposed website copy, not a statement of verified credentials or client results. Counters describe the valuation approach, not historical company performance. Confirm service scope and replace provisional privacy/terms notices with approved company policies before launch. Images are AI-generated editorial illustrations, not actual ValPal offices or employees.
